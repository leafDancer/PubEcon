## Interpretation

- Crossref is the primary record. OpenAlex supplies DOI-keyed enrichment and may supply a missing byline; those rows are marked `openalex_backfill` and never replace a Crossref author.
- Journal is a first-class dimension. Duplicate detection is scoped by journal, so identical titles in different journals are not merged.
- AER P&P records are included through 2017. The P&P flag is not applied to other journals.
- ORCID events are used only after identity validation against the author's papers. Unverified links keep their identity record but their events are withheld.
- Missing data stays missing. No stage is inferred from age, seniority, or a current title when the dated evidence does not cover the paper.
